My Dearest,

I have been thinking about the things that happened between us, and I find myself searching for the right words to express what my heart truly wishes to say.

I am sorry for the times I have hurt you, especially when I should have been more thoughtful of your feelings. I know that saying sorry cannot simply erase the pain I have caused, and I do not expect a few words to make everything right again.

If there is one thing I wish you to understand, it is that your feelings matter to me. I never want you to feel as though your hurt is unimportant or that your patience is something I may take for granted.

I know that an apology means little if the same mistakes continue to happen. That is why I want to learn from this, to listen more carefully, to be more mindful of my actions, and to show through my efforts what my words alone cannot prove.

I cannot promise that I will always be perfect, but I can promise to take responsibility when I fall short and to keep trying to become someone who makes you feel loved, respected, and understood.

I do not write this letter to pressure you to forgive me immediately. I only hope that, in time, my sincerity will be seen not merely in the words written upon this paper, but in the way I choose to treat you each day.

Thank you for the place you hold in my life. I am truly sorry for the hurt I have caused, and I hope I may have the chance to make things better, one sincere effort at a time.

With all my sincerity,
Yours, always
